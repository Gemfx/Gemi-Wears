GEMI WEARS is a premium Nigerian streetwear brand built around shared purpose.

The four-hands mark is our starting point.

It represents unity, trust, loyalty and strength—four values that guide what we make and how we show up. GEMI WEARS is for the people committed to creating something greater than themselves.
